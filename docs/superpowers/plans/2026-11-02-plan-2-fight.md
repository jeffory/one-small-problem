# One Small Problem, Plan 2: Fight Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The walkable Moon becomes a fight. You grab the flag pole, beat the first skitterer for its plasma pistol, and fight seeded encounters of skitterers, floaters and a rim brute (design A). The fight has plasma bolts that light the ground, goo arcing in low gravity, scorch marks and chain-reacting canisters, plus health, cells, death and a restart at the wreck. It all still ships as one packed HTML file well under 131,072 bytes. The plan opens with the debt carried over from Plan 1.

**Architecture:**
- **Generation:** `src/gen/encounters.js` plans encounters, pickups and canisters along the route, under the same determinism rules as all generation. The plan is attached to the world as `world.plan` and is included in the checksum.
- **Simulation:** `src/game/{weapons,bolts,nav,rigs,aliens,fight}.js` are pure modules with no GPU or DOM access.
  - `main.js` drives them in its real-time sub-steps.
  - All randomness comes from a seeded rng, so Node tests can run whole fights.
- **Look:**
  - `src/game/models.js` builds every mesh on the CPU. The aliens are a port of look-test design A, and the guns are lathed and rounded.
  - `src/game/fightView.js` turns the fight state into Frame fields.
  - The renderer gains four things: an instanced sprite pass (additive plasma glows and opaque goo droplets), a first-person view-model pass, a per-mesh hit flash, and a post tint.
- **UI:** a Quake-style status line, a crosshair, prompts and pickup messages.

This is plan 2 of 4.
- **Plan 3 (the base):** crater descent, base generation and themed rooms. It also places the golf balls and pins the golden checksums.
- **Plan 4 (show):** audio, radio, opening, title screen and attract bot, ending, end screen, Esc pause and settings.

**Not in this plan:**
- Golf balls (Plan 3).
- Radio lines (Plan 4). This plan emits `F.events` so Plan 4 can hook into them.
- The attract bot (Plan 4). It will drive the `Input` shape defined here.
- The opening and lander break-up (Plan 4). Here the lander stays intact and the flag stands planted beside it.

**Tech Stack:**
- Same as Plan 1: Node 22, esbuild 0.28.2, terser 5.51.2, jspackcompress 0.1.1, @playwright/test 1.64.1.
- `node:test` for unit tests.
- WebGL2 / GLSL ES 3.00.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-02-one-small-problem-design.md`. The relevant sections are §4.3–4.6, §5, §6.2, §6.5–6.7 and §6.10.

**Visual reference:** `spikes/look-test.html` (design A, line-up scene 3). Port its alien, gun and material recipes and its constants, but not its JS structure.

## Global Constraints

**Carried from Plan 1 unchanged**
- **Output:** one self-contained `.html` file of at most **131,072 bytes** on disk. The build fails over the cap.
- **Runs from:** `https://` (GitHub Pages sends `charset=utf-8`) and from `file://`. It makes no network requests except the universal decoder's re-fetch of its own file.
- **Packing:** pack with `universalDecoder: true, compactHtml: false`, and keep the `<!doctype html>`.
- **Graphics:** WebGL2 only, with a half-float colour target via `EXT_color_buffer_float` and an RGBA8 fallback.
- **Code and dependencies:** plain JavaScript, with no runtime dependencies. Every file under `src/`, `tools/` and `build.mjs` must be 7-bit ASCII; use `\u` escapes in strings.
- **Determinism in `src/gen/**`:**
  - Allowed: `+ - * /`, bit operations, and `Math.imul/sqrt/floor/ceil/round/trunc/min/max/abs`.
  - Never: `Math.sin/cos/tan/asin/acos/atan/atan2/pow/exp/expm1/log*/hypot/cbrt/random`, `**`, `Date`, or `.sort(`.
  - The same seed gives byte-identical world data in every browser and in Node.
- **Coordinates:**
  - Units are metres, and y is up.
  - The player faces −z at yaw 0. Forward is `(−sin yaw, −cos yaw)`, and `f = (−sin yaw·cos pitch, sin pitch, −cos yaw·cos pitch)`.
  - Height-field sample `(i, j)` sits at world `(i − 256, j − 256)`.
- **Seeds:** the URL hash is normalised to uppercase `[A-Z0-9-]`, at most 24 characters. With no usable hash, the seed is a random `WORD-NNNN` from the 32-word list. New in Task 1: text with non-ASCII characters becomes `X-` plus 8 hex digits.
- **Look:**
  - 270 px internal height (180 and 360 as options), nearest-neighbour upscale.
  - 51-colour palette with 4×4 Bayer dither.
  - 256² textures, `NEAREST` magnification, `NEAREST_MIPMAP_LINEAR` minification.
  - Sun intensity 1.55, curvature `1/1800`, vertical FOV 62°.
- **Naming:** only `_`-prefixed properties get mangled. Names read from outside the bundle (`window.ospTest`, GLSL uniforms) must not be prefixed.

**New for Plan 2**
- **Simulation is pure:** `src/game/{weapons,bolts,nav,rigs,aliens,fight,models}.js` never touch `document.`, `window.`, `gl.`, `performance.now`, `Date.now` or `Math.random`. Node tests import them directly. They may use trig, because they are not generation.
- **Seeded randomness:** all game randomness comes from `makeRng(world.seed + '/sim')`. The same world, the same inputs and the same `dt` sequence must give the same fight. Tests and Plan 4's attract bot rely on this.
- **Sub-steps:** the sim runs in `main.js`'s real-time sub-steps, at most 0.05 s each, with a stall cap of 0.25 s per frame. One-shot inputs (`use`, `weapon`, `wheel`) apply on the frame's first sub-step only.
- **Alien yaw is the look test's convention:** an alien with yaw `a` faces `(sin a, 0, cos a)`, and turning toward a point uses `atan2(px − x, pz − z)`. This is not the player's convention.
- **Per-frame render caps:** 16 point lights, 8 sphere occluders and 512 sprites. Anything beyond is dropped, and the candidates nearest the camera are kept.
- **Tuning tables:** `WEAPONS`, `ALIENS`, `PICKUP`, `CANISTER` and `budgetAt` hold the starting tuning [call]. Tests read these constants rather than repeating the numbers, so retuning after the hands-on check doesn't break tests. The exceptions are structural facts such as "5 bolts" or "brutes only from p 0.3".
- **Size:** expect about 26–36 KB after this plan. If the build passes 48 KB, record a ruling with the cause.

## Review Focus

1. **Dying mid-chaos.** If you die with bolts in flight, a canister fuse lit and goo falling, the restart must start clean: no stale bolt, explosion or goo, and full health. Tested in Task 8.
2. **A crowded fight,** such as a canister chain in the middle of an encounter. Lights, sphere occluders and sprites must clip to 16, 8 and 512, keeping the nearest, and must never overflow their arrays or throw. Tested in Task 10.
3. **Low or uneven frame rates (20–144 fps).** Bolts must not tunnel through aliens or the player, and aliens must move at the same pace at every rate. Tested in Tasks 5 and 7.
4. **Weapon keys and the wheel for weapons you don't own, or holding fire with no cells.** Nothing should break: an empty weapon falls back to the flag pole, and the wheel only cycles through weapons you own. Tested in Task 5.
5. **The click that captures the mouse must not fire, and weapon keys must work without pointer lock.** Tested in Task 11.

**Interfaces:**
- **Produces, from `surface.js`:** `BERM = 40`, `BERM_H = 30`, and `bermLift(de) => number`.
  - `bermLift(de) = BERM_H·t²`, where `t = smoothstep(BERM, 0, de)`.
  - `de` is a sample's distance from the nearest map edge in samples: `min(i, j, N−1−i, N−1−j)`.
- **Applying the berm:** the last step of `buildHeightfield`, after the lander flattening, adds `bermLift(de) * (0.75 + 0.5 * valueNoise2(x / 9, z / 9, s3))` to every sample. This makes no new rng draws.
- **Ruling:** the route is clamped to ±216 (`de` ≥ 40) and the lander sits at `de` ≥ 66, so the berm touches neither. Checksums change. `smoke.spec.mjs` computes the Node checksum at run time, so nothing pinned breaks.
- **Ruling for `#ЛУНА`:** the spec says any text works [call]. `normalizeSeed` keeps its decode step. Then, if the decoded text contains any character above U+007E, it returns `'X-' + xmur3(text)().toString(16).toUpperCase().padStart(8, '0')`. Everything else behaves as before. Because `main.js` already writes the normalised seed back with `replaceState`, the shared link stays ASCII.
- **Produces, from `tests/helpers.mjs`:** `edgeMargin(world, bound = HALF − MAP_MARGIN, eye = 1.7) => number`. This is the worst value, across all four sides, of the edge sample's terrain height minus (the ground at the movement bound on the same row or column plus `eye`). A result of 0 or more means a player standing at the bound looks up at the crest and can never see past the end of the map.

```js
import { sampleGround, HALF, N, MAP_MARGIN } from '../src/gen/surface.js';
export function edgeMargin(w, bound = HALF - MAP_MARGIN, eye = 1.7) {
  let worst = Infinity;
  for (let k = HALF - bound; k <= HALF + bound; k++) {
    const c = k - HALF;
    for (const [e, g] of [[w.h[k * N], sampleGround(w, -bound, c)], [w.h[k * N + N - 1], sampleGround(w, bound, c)],
      [w.h[k], sampleGround(w, c, -bound)], [w.h[(N - 1) * N + k], sampleGround(w, c, bound)]]) worst = Math.min(worst, e - g - eye);
  }
  return worst;
}
```

- **Consumes:** Plan 1's `PLAYER`, `createPlayer` and `stepPlayer`.
- **Produces:**
  - `createPlayer` also sets `kx: 0, kz: 0`, the knock velocity.
  - `stepPlayer(p, input, dt, ground, bound, g = PLAYER.gSurface)` uses `g` for gravity. The base will pass about 9.

### Task 4: Encounter plan (generation)

**Files:**
- Create: `src/gen/encounters.js`, `tests/encounters.test.mjs`
- Modify: `src/gen/passable.js`, `src/gen/surface.js`, `src/gen/checksum.js`, `tests/seeds.soak.mjs`

**Interfaces:**
- **Consumes:** the world from `generateSurface`, `makeRng`, `sampleGround`, `polylineLength`, `N`, `HALF`, `MAP_MARGIN` and `MAX_STEP`.
- **Produces, from `passable.js`:**
  - `reachMap(grid, n, start, maxStep, margin = 0) => Uint8Array`, where 1 means the cell is reachable from `start` by 4-neighbour steps with |Δ| ≤ `maxStep`, inside the margin.
  - `passable` becomes `reachMap(...)[goal index] === 1`.
- **Produces, from `surface.js`:**
  - `flagSpot(world) => {x, z}`. This is the lander's local (−4.5, 3) turned to face the route, computed without trig as `{x: L.x − 4.5·dz + 3·dx, z: L.z + 4.5·dx + 3·dz}`, where `(dx, dz)` is `world.spawn.dx/dz`.
  - `generateSurface` sets `world.plan = planFight(world)` once a world is accepted.
- **Produces, from `checksum.js`:** the checksum also hashes the plan as one `Float32Array`:
  - every encounter's `x, z, p`, then each alien's type code (`sk` 0, `fl` 1, `br` 2) and its `x, y, z`
  - every pickup's kind code (`tape` 0, `cells` 1) and its `x, y, z`
  - every canister's `x, y, z`
- **Produces, from `encounters.js`:**
  - `COST = {sk: 1, fl: 2, br: 4}`, `SURFACE_P = 0.35`, `FIRST_AT = 70`, `GAP = 60`
  - `budgetAt(p) = Math.floor(2 + 16·p)`, which is 2 at the start and 7 at the rim
  - `planFight(world) => FightPlan`, where:
    ```
    FightPlan = {
      encounters: [{x, z, p, first, aliens: [{type: 'sk'|'fl'|'br', x, y, z}]}],
      pickups:    [{kind: 'tape'|'cells', x, y, z}],
      canisters:  [{x, y, z}],
    }
    ```
- **Rules for `planFight`:**
  1. **Inputs:**
     - `rng = makeRng(world.seed + '/fight/' + world.attempt)`
     - `reach = reachMap(world.occ, N, spawnCell, MAX_STEP, MAP_MARGIN)`
     - A spot is *standable* when:
       - `|x|` and `|z|` are at most `HALF − MAP_MARGIN − 4`
       - `reach` is 1 at its cell, `(Math.round(x + HALF), Math.round(z + HALF))`
       - `occ − h < 0.05` there, so it isn't on a rock
       - every ±1 m neighbour in x and z differs in `occ` by at most `MAX_STEP`
  2. **Positions along the route:** `Lr = polylineLength(route)`. `routeAt(s)` returns the point and unit direction at arc length `s`, clamped to `[0, Lr]`.
  3. **Encounter spacing:**
     - `count = max(5, floor((Lr − FIRST_AT) / GAP))`.
     - Encounter `k` (for `k` from 0 to `count`) sits at `s_k = FIRST_AT + k·(Lr − 8 − FIRST_AT)/count`, with `p = SURFACE_P·s_k/Lr`.
     - Its centre `{x, z}` is `routeAt(s_k + 12)`.
  4. **Composition:**
     - Encounter 0 is exactly one `sk`, with `first: true`.
     - Every other encounter starts from the budget `B = budgetAt(p)`:
       - if `p ≥ 0.3` and `B ≥ 4`, add one `br` (`B −= 4`)
       - if `B ≥ 2` and (`k` is odd or `rng.next() < 0.35`), add one `fl` (`B −= 2`)
       - if `B ≥ 4` and `rng.next() < 0.3`, add a second `fl`
       - fill the rest of the budget with `sk`
  5. **Placement:**
     - Each alien tries up to 40 candidates at `centre + side·rng.range(−14, 14) + dir·rng.range(−4, 10)`. Encounter 0 uses `rng.range(−4, 4)` for the side offset.
     - It accepts the first standable candidate at least 2.5 m from the encounter's other aliens.
     - If none qualifies, it falls back to the route centreline at `routeAt(s_k + 12 + 2j)` for alien index `j`.
     - Walkers stand at `y = sampleGround`. Floaters hover at `sampleGround + rng.range(3, 4.5)`.
  6. **Pickups** use the same candidate and fallback rule, with `y = ground`:

  4. **Floater** (`'hover'`):
     - It eases `y` toward `ground + hover` at 2 m/s.
     - It moves outward when nearer than `keep[0]`, inward when farther than `keep[1]`, and tangentially (circling) otherwise, at `speed`.

  5. **Brute** (`'chase'` at `speed` along the field):
     - **Claw:** within `claw` m it enters `'claw'`. `claw` winds 0→1 over 0.4 s. Then, if the player is still within `claw + .5`, it calls `ctx.melee(a, clawDmg, clawKnock)`. Then `'recover'` for 1.0 s.

     - **After firing:** with probability `staggerChance` (from `ctx.rng`) it enters `'stagger'`: it lurches 1.5 m forward over 0.3 s and can't act for 1.0 s. Otherwise it enters `'recover'` for `recover` s.
  6. **Death:** a blow that takes `hp` to 0 or below sets `'dead'`. `fall` eases 0→1 over 0.5 s, and floaters drop to the ground at 3 m/s². Dead aliens never move or shoot on their own.
  7. **`hurtAlien`:**
     - `hp −= dmg`, `flash = 1`
     - it alerts the alien and its encounter
